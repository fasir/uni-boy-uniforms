About Uni-Boy Uniforms

Built on experience.
Made for everyday.

Established under the Uni-Boy name in 2017 and backed by 30 years of industry experience, Uni-Boy Uniforms serves the educational, corporate, hospitality and service sectors. With over 100 employees and a monthly production capacity of 20,000 garments, our business operates across three core divisions:

01

Retail Store

Our retail store offers ready-to-wear uniforms in a range of sizes, making it easy for customers to find a suitable fit. For those who need a fit beyond our standard sizes, we offer custom stitching tailored to their requirements. From fabric selection to fit and finishing, we prioritise the wearer’s comfort, creating uniforms that allow easy movement throughout the day.

02

Readymade Uniform Wholesale

We supply readymade uniform pants, student coats, shirts and other garments to retailers in the uniform business, supporting them with products that meet their customers’ needs.

03

Bulk Uniform Stitching

We undertake bulk uniform stitching using materials supplied by customers, tailoring production to their required designs, patterns, sizes and specifications.

Across all three divisions, the comfort of the person wearing our uniforms comes first. We combine practical experience with evolving patterns and trends to deliver garments that offer comfort, durability and a professional appearance.
